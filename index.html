Sepsis

What is Sepsis?

Sepsis is a serious medical condition caused by the body's extreme response to an infection.

Symptoms

  • Fever or low body temperature
  • Fast heartbeat
  • Fast breathing
  • Confusion
  • Extreme weakness

Causes

Sepsis can develop from infections such as pneumonia, urinary tract infections, and skin infections.

Prevention

  • Wash your hands regularly.
  • Keep wounds clean.
  • Get recommended vaccinations.
  • Treat infections early.

Important

Sepsis is a medical emergency. Seek medical help immediately if you suspect sepsis.
